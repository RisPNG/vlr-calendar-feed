How to subscribe

Subscribe by URL

  1. Choose a calendar and copy its subscription link.
  2. In your calendar app, find the option to add a calendar by URL.
  3. Paste the link and subscribe.

In Google Calendar, use Other calendars > + > From URL.

Schedule updates

Subscriptions receive schedule changes and results. Your calendar app decides when to check for updates.

Downloading an ICS file gives you a one-time import. Subscribe by URL to receive later changes.
